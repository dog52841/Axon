🔥 2. wait(seconds) tool → YES, but refine it

Your idea is right, but raw wait(180) has issues:

blocks unnecessarily
doesn’t wake on events
wastes time if something happens early
Upgrade it to:

⚡ event-aware waiting

Instead of:
wait(180)
Do:
wait({
  duration: 180,
  until: "email_reply || webhook || task_complete"
})

👉 This means:

wake early if something happens
otherwise sleep
🧠 Even better abstraction

Call it:

wait_for
Examples:
wait_for(email_reply, timeout=180)
wait_for(build_complete)
wait_for(payment_event)

👉 This is MUCH smarter than blind sleeping
